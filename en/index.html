OUR STORY

Experience Fethiye
to the fullest.

Experience the tranquility of Fethiye. Inspired by the unique nature of Fethiye and the warm atmosphere of the Mediterranean, ROOMS 365 offers a special accommodation experience that combines comfort and simplicity. We aim to create an environment where our guests do more than stay; they relax, create beautiful memories and enjoy every moment of their holiday. With our modern, thoughtfully designed rooms, peaceful atmosphere and guest-focused service, ROOMS 365 is a place where you can feel at home. For us, a beautiful holiday is found in the little details.

When designing this hotel, we had one thing in mind: giving everyone who comes here a little more time for themselves, for peace and for beautiful moments.
